Aggregation existing answers

Fréchet mean
$\theta_{t+1}\leftarrow\arg\min_{\theta\in\mathcal{M}}\tfrac1k\sum_i d(\theta,\theta_t^{(i)})^2$
Issue: no closed form, only an iterative flow.
KFAvg
$\theta_{t+1}\leftarrow\exp_{\theta_t}\Bigl(\tfrac1k\sum_i \log_{\theta_t}(\theta_t^{(i)})\Bigr)$
Issue: $\exp$ and $\log$ often unknown or expensive.
RetAvg
$\theta_{t+1}\leftarrow R_{\theta_t}\Bigl(\tfrac1k\sum_i R^{-1}_{\theta_t}(\theta_t^{(i)})\Bigr)$
Issue: $R$ and $R^{-1}$ rarely both available.
Étape 1: l'argmin, montré par le flot de Karcher qui converge. Puis une seule itération de ce flot, c'est KFAvg. Puis RetAvg, exp et log remplacés par R et son inverse.

Aggregation what we propose

A useful barycentre need not be the Fréchet mean.

RLAvg
$\theta_{t+1}\leftarrow R_{\theta_t}\Bigl(\tfrac1k\sum_i L_{\theta_t}(\theta_t^{(i)})\Bigr) =\Pi\Bigl(\theta_t+\tfrac1k\sum_i L_{\theta_t}(\theta_t^{(i)})\Bigr)$
Idea: replace $R^{-1}$ by the lifting $L_{\theta_t}(\theta)=P_{\theta_t}(\theta-\theta_t)$.
ProjAvg
$\theta_{t+1}\leftarrow\Pi\Bigl(\tfrac1k\sum_i\theta_t^{(i)}\Bigr)$
Idea: average in the ambient space, then project back.
A natural choice for $\Pi$ is the nearest-point projection, but any projection works. On Stiefel that is $\mathrm{uf}$, and a QR factor does too.
Mentionner les r-barycentres de Bouchard et al. Insister sur le coût: une projection, la même que celle du pas local.
