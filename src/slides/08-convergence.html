Convergence assumptions

Projection
$\Pi(y)=\arg\min_{\theta\in\mathcal{M}}\lVert y-\theta\rVert$
Local solver, RSGD
$\theta_{j+1}=\Pi\bigl(\theta_j-\alpha\,\tilde{\operatorname{grad}}f(\theta_j,\mathcal{B}_j)\bigr)$
Aggregation
$\Pi\bigl(\Delta_t\bigr)$ or $\Pi\bigl(\theta_t+\Delta_t\bigr)$
ProjAvg and RLAvg, same rate
Proximal smoothness
Both stages evaluate $\Pi$ off the manifold, so $\Pi$ must be a map there. A compact submanifold has a tube of radius $\gamma$ around it on which the nearest point is unique.
Smoothness, per sample
Every individual loss has Lipschitz ambient gradient. Per sample, not per client, because clipping acts before averaging.
Bounded minibatch dispersion
Within a client, the per-sample gradients do not spread arbitrarily far from the client's own gradient.
Bounded heterogeneity
Across clients, the local gradients do not spread arbitrarily far from the global one.

Compactness also gives a finite objective range and a uniform gradient bound $G$.

Faire glisser y. Dans le tube, une seule projection. Au centre, deux, et Pi cesse d'être une application. C'est tout ce que la régularité proximale demande.

Convergence the rate

$\displaystyle \frac1T\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\mathbb{E}\bigl\lVert\operatorname{grad}F(\theta_t)\bigr\rVert^2 = O\!\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}\Bigl[\,1 + \tfrac1k\tfrac{n-k}{n-1}\,\bigl(\text{heterogeneity}+\text{clipping spread}\bigr)^2 + \tfrac{1}{k\tau}\bigl(\text{minibatch noise}+d_{\mathcal{M}}\cdot\text{DP noise}\bigr)\Bigr] + \ \text{clipping bias}^2\right)$
Optimisation, $O(1/\sqrt{T})$
The usual nonconvex stationarity rate. Free of $k$, $\tau$ and of the privacy parameters.
Heterogeneity
The price of partial participation. Vanishes at full participation, largest with a single client per round. Clipping adds its own spread to it.
Minibatch noise
Damped by the cohort and by the local trajectory, and removed entirely at full local batches.
DP noise
The dominant privacy cost, damped the same way but only removable by weaker privacy. It scales with the intrinsic dimension $d_{\mathcal{M}}$, which is what the projection design buys.
Clipping bias, a floor
The only term that does not decay. The iterates reach a neighbourhood of stationarity, not a stationary point, unless the clip is inactive.
Two readings
A larger clip shrinks the floor but needs more noise for the same privacy. At a fixed budget the cost is free of $\tau$, so local work costs nothing in privacy terms.
Ne pas lire la formule. Dire d'abord qu'elle se coupe en deux: une partie qui s'évanouit et un plancher. Puis les cinq termes. Insister sur d_M contre la dimension ambiante, et sur le compromis clip contre bruit.

What is new
The two regimes hold together for the first time with a privacy guarantee. The projection-based linearisation is what makes them accessible at once, without curvature-dependent constants.
What it costs
Compactness. PriRFed covers general manifolds, ours does not. In exchange the geometry enters through one map instead of a retraction and its inverse.
Dire clairement le compromis: on perd la généralité de la variété, on gagne le régime réaliste et une seule opération géométrique. Sur Stiefel, R et son inverse ne sont pas disponibles ensemble en forme close, ce qui rend le point concret.
