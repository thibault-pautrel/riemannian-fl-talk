How to classify a covariance matrix?

Naive idea
Classify $\mathrm{vec}(\Sigma)\in\R^{C^2}$
Issue. High dimension. The symmetry is ignored and the SPD constraint is not exploited.
Refined idea
Classify $\mathrm{Vech}(\Sigma)\in\R^{C(C+1)/2}$
Issue. Half the dimension. The positive definite constraint is still ignored.
Main idea
Exploit the geometry of SPD matrices, with a Riemannian architecture.
With C = 22 channels, vec gives 484 coordinates and Vech gives 253. Recall the Euclidean segment on the cone. Then announce SPDNet.

SPDNet Huang and Van Gool, 2017

Vue d'ensemble d'abord. Puis un zoom par couche. BiMap réduit la dimension, ReEig rectifie le spectre, LogEig aplatit vers l'espace tangent en I. Terminer en insistant : une seule couche porte des paramètres.

SPDNet: What is optimised?

Layers without learnable parameters
ReEig
spectral rectification, a fixed map
LogEig
tangent space at $I$, a fixed map
Vech
upper triangle of the matrix, a fixed linear map into $\mathbb{R}^{d_L(d_L+1)/2}$
Parametrised layers
BiMap1..L
$X_k = W_k\,X_{k-1}\,W_k^{\top}$
$W_k\in\mathrm{St}(d_{k-1},d_k)$, orthonormal columns. One weight per BiRe block, so $L$ of them.
Fully connected
$\hat{y}=\mathrm{softmax}\bigl(\xi\,\mathrm{Vech}(X_L)+\beta\bigr)$
$\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times d_L(d_L+1)/2}$ and $\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{K}$, free parameters with no constraint.
Consequence
Two optimisers in the same model. Riemannian on Stiefel for the $W_k$, and plain Euclidean for $\xi$ and $\beta$.
