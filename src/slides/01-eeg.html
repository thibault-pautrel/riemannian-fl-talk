EEG data

Electrodes record the electrical activity of the brain.

Motor imagery: the subject imagines a movement and the signal should tell us which one.

A trial is a matrix $X\in\mathbb{R}^{C\times T}$, with $C$ channels and $T$ timestamps, carrying a label $y$: right hand, left hand, feet, tongue, rest.

Neighbouring electrodes see related signals. That redundancy is the structure we want to exploit.

Say that C is small, around 22, and T is large. Click one electrode to show that a single channel says almost nothing on its own.

From raw signals to covariance

The channels are correlated. Important information to predict the class: how pairs of electrodes co-vary over the trial.

Centre, then accumulate the product for one pair, then fill the matrix. Click a cell to change the pair live. Cite Barachant 2012.

SPD matrices

Covariance matrices are structured features.

Definition
$\Sigma\in\mathbb{R}^{C\times C}$ is symmetric positive definite if $\Sigma^{\top}=\Sigma$ and $x^{\top}\Sigma x>0$ for every $x\neq 0$.

An open convex cone: stable under positive scaling, unbounded, and its boundary $\det\Sigma=0$ does not belong to it.

Not a vector space.

What model can we train on these features?

Drag a point toward the boundary and let them watch the determinant fall toward zero. That is the picture to keep for the whole talk.
