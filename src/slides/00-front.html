Three obstacles

  • 1 Geometry. EEG features are covariance matrices. They live on a curved set, and $\theta$ does too.
  • 2 Distribution. The data sits in separate sites and cannot be pooled.
  • 3 Privacy. Exchanging model parameters is not, on its own, a guarantee.

The geometry part is a tutorial. No prerequisite.
